← Ask SIPAEmbed on a manufacturer site

Bryan's idea: one tool, built once, that every SIPA manufacturer can put on its own website. This page shows how that works. Paste one line into the site and the Ask SIPA button appears in the corner. Usage is counted per manufacturer so SIPA can see it is being used.

The one line


  
Change data-mfr to the company name and data-color to the brand color. That is the whole install.

Why this is the win-win Bryan described

  

    
For SIPA: one engine, one document set to maintain, usage numbers by manufacturer, and a log of questions the Best Practices do not answer yet.

    
For each manufacturer: a modern installer helper on their own site for a one-time contribution, no software to run, and their own details can be layered on top of the SIPA baseline later.

    
For the installer: a plain answer with the page it came from, on a phone, on the jobsite.
